# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in this project to be respectful, constructive, and welcoming.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful of different backgrounds, experience levels, and viewpoints.
- Give technical feedback about the work rather than the person.
- Prefer reproducible evidence and clear reasoning when discussing defects.
- Respect privacy; do not post QR payloads containing credentials or personal data.
- Help keep reviews focused on project goals: privacy, reliability, accessibility, security, and maintainability.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate disruption, publishing private information, or other conduct that makes collaboration unsafe is not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other participation that violates this code, and may restrict participation when necessary.
